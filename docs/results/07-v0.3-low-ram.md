# 07. v0.3 under low free RAM (roadmap step 2)

Measured on the owner's laptop on 22 Nov 2026, 20:37 to 21:40 IST, **on AC** (checked before every run). Runtime v0.3 (commit 31a1c9f), Gemma 3n E2B unless noted. Same method and scripts as [06-low-ram.md](06-low-ram.md). Raw data: `bench\out\lowram_v03\`.

**The runs overlapped with a jitmo audit run** (`audit-copy.exe` starting 6 to 8 `jitmo-model-worker.exe` at a time, about 0.4 to 0.7 GB). The owner chose to treat it as real background load. Its process count, CPU and RAM were logged once a second (`bench\proclog.ps1`, `procs.jsonl` in each run folder). The audit ran from before 20:37 until about 21:25, so the 20% sets ran with it and most of the 15% sets ran without it. Free RAM moved between 0.1 and 4.8 GB.

**Measured** = numbers from these runs. **Reasoned** = inferred, not measured.

## Load estimates, v0.2 vs v0.3 (measured)

Default budget (`reserve_free_mb` 1536). "Real" is the child after a 128-token answer (MiniLM: after 5 embedding calls). The peak equalled the final value in every case.

| Model | v0.2 estimate MB | v0.3 estimate MB | Real working set MB | Real private MB | v0.3 estimate ÷ working set | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-4B | 3981 | 3533 | – | – | – | refused (3511 MB free) |
| Gemma 3n E2B | 2663 | 2279 | 1578 | 371 | 1.44× | loaded (it was refused once at 921 MB free, during an audit spike) |
| gemma-3-270m router | 1265 | 705 | 334 | 146 | 2.1× | loaded |
| Qwen3.5-0.8B | 1596 | 1068 | 714 | 657 | 1.50× | loaded |
| MiniLM (embedding) | – | 536 | 37 | 126 | 4.3× of private | loaded |

- v0.3 estimates are 11 to 44% lower than v0.2 and still always above real use.
- MiniLM is the one model still well over-estimated (536 MB for about 130 MB real).
- **Gemma 3n was still refused in the pacer sets** at 1.7 to 2.4 GB free (2279 + 512 reserve). Its real need is about 1.6 GB, so some of these refusals were too strict.
- During the audit, tok/s in the load test fell a lot: the 0.8B ran at 9.9 tok/s (33.5 without the audit), the router at 15.2 (86.3), and Gemma 3n at 3.8 (12.9).

## Pacer v0.3: 20% vs 15% (measured)

Probe = the "user app": 20 s of a CPU task, then 20 s over a 200 MB buffer. Medians of 3 repeats (bgload at 15: 2 repeats). The no-pacer row was run right next to each set.

| Set | Conditions | Alone: task / pass ms | No pacer: task / pass slowdown, tok/s | Pacer: task / pass slowdown, tok/s | Pacer + 20% load: task / pass slowdown, tok/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| light 20 (default) | audit running: total CPU median 96%, free RAM 0.6 to 4.2 GB | 8.31 / 27.3 | +26% / −3%, 4.49 | +31% / +8%, 2.10 | +61% / 0%, 1.27 |
| light 15 | audit ended early in the set, free RAM 0.4 to 4.6 GB | 7.29 / 21.4 | +29% / +28%, 6.96 | +7% / +21%, 3.87 | +45% / +28%, 2.81 |

Levels and flapping:

| Set | Levels during the probe | Level changes per 40 s window |
|---|---|---|
| 20, pacer | heavy 100% (others' CPU 72 to 95%) | 0 / 0 / 0 |
| 20, pacer + load | heavy 55 to 100% (none after a pressure unload) | 0 / 0 / 0 |
| 15, pacer | light 0 to 64%, heavy 36 to 100% | 2 / 0 / 0 |
| 15, pacer + load | heavy 100% | 0 / 0 |

Background load alone, light 15, 1 repeat, audit over (free RAM 3.0 to 4.6 GB, 29 s windows):

| Load | tok/s | Levels | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| none | 5.69 | light 10%, heavy 90% (others' CPU median 21%) | 1 |
| ~20% | 6.37 | light 40%, heavy 60% | 1 |
| ~45% | 4.73 | heavy 100% | 0 |

- **Flapping is fixed.** v0.2 under low RAM changed level 3 to 9 times per 40 s. v0.3 changed 0 to 2 times per 40 s, and about once per 29 s background window.
- The "holding" reasons appear as designed, for example `holding: other processes used over 15% CPU, now 11%`.
- **20 vs 15 cannot be compared fairly here.** With the audit running, others' CPU was 60 to 100%, so the pacer sat at heavy whatever the threshold. The 15 set ran mostly after the audit ended.
- With the audit, the memory-heavy probe phase barely slowed (−3% to +8%) because the PC was already saturated. The "alone" baseline was itself slow: 27.3 ms per pass, against 21.4 without the audit.
- The 20% background-load set is invalid. The model was unloaded for memory pressure about 1 minute in, and later loads were refused at 1.7 to 2.4 GB free.
- **Reasoned:** the 20% default should matter on a quiet PC (idle at 12 to 13%). This was not measurable in this session.

## Forced Qwen3-4B, "does it freeze?" (measured, 1 run)

`bench\run_lowram_forced.sh` with memhog first lowering free RAM to about 3 GB (an 8 GB laptop with apps open). The load estimate was bypassed (`load_estimate_mb = 1`, bench config only). The audit was not running.

Windows counters, one row per second from the load request (`typeperf`, `faults.csv`):

| Second | Available MB | Page reads/s | Pages input/s |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2171 | 3239 | 15,125 |
| 2 | 2190 | 939 | 5,150 |
| 3 | 1454 | 764 | 3,740 |
| 4 | 1536 | 2872 | 301,329 |
| 5 | 1063 | 6683 | 71,919 |
| 6 | 888 | 6882 | 75,455 |
| 7 | 475 | 7280 | 71,115 |
| 8 | **250: safety stop** (ramlog read 270 MB) | 6628 | 68,625 |

- **The safety stop fired during the load, before any token.** Free RAM fell under 300 MB within about 8 s. Disk page reads rose to about 7,000 per second.
- The guard killed the runtime and its llama-server. memhog freed its memory at the same moment, and free RAM went back to 2.9 GB.
- So forcing a 4B model into about 3 GB free pushes the PC into heavy paging. **The runtime's refusal (3533 MB needed) was right.**
- The foreground lag and tok/s under the forced model were not reached. "Probe alone" just before (with about 3 GB free) was 6.7 ms per task and 28.3 ms per pass.

## Memory pressure (measured)

**On purpose (1 run).** Gemma 3n generated, memhog lowered free RAM to about 750 MB and held it for 30 s, and the probe ran during the hold.

| Time (IST) | Event |
|---|---|
| 21:38:53 | First token, 2.8 GB free |
| 21:39:02 | memhog starts. Free RAM falls to 600 to 760 MB |
| 21:39:04 | "memory pressure (705 MB free …): may finish its generation (at most 15 s)" |
| 21:39:19 | "waited 15 s … cancelling 1 request(s) and unloading it now" |
| 21:39:19 | The client gets the stream error `memory_pressure`: "free RAM stayed low for 15 s. Close some apps and try again". 236 tokens were done |
| 21:39:20 | Model unloaded (peak working set 1578 MB, private 370 MB) |

- The 15 s limit fires as designed: it cancelled after 15.2 s, the client got a clear error, and the model was unloaded in 0.4 s.
- **The PC stayed responsive.** During the hold the probe's frame lateness p99 was 0.76 ms (alone: about 0.9 ms). The CPU task took 9.1 ms and the 200 MB pass 25.8 ms, about +25% and +21% while the model was still generating.

**In real runs (with the audit), 7 unloads:**

| Run | Event |
|---|---|
| Probe 20, adaptive and bgload | 3 pressure unloads. Two waited for the generation to end (17 s and 23 s, the bench client ended it). For the 23 s one, free RAM probably went back above 1024 MB for a moment, which allows a finish (reasoned). One was cancelled at 17 s ("waited 17 s … cancelling") |
| No-pacer background set (first try) | 1 pressure unload, 1 s after the generation ended |
| No-pacer background set (second try) | **2 critical unloads**: at 113 MB free and at 462 MB free. Running generations failed at once, as designed |
| Background set, light 20 | 1 pressure unload of an idle model (immediate) |

- The critical path (under 500 MB) now has a real-world test. It fired twice and unloaded within about 1 s.
- **Reasoned:** the 15 s limit can show as 15 to 17 s, because the watchdog checks once a second and pressure needs two low checks.

## Recommendations

1. **Keep the v0.3 pacer rules.** Flapping went from 3 to 9 to 0 to 2 changes per 40 s, with clear "holding" reasons.
2. **Keep refusing Qwen3-4B with about 3.5 GB or less free.** Forcing it made free RAM fall under 300 MB in about 8 s.
3. **Trim the estimate a little more for small models and MiniLM.** MiniLM is 4.3× real use, and the router 2.1×. Gemma 3n is still refused at 1.7 to 2.4 GB free while it needs about 1.6 GB. Consider `reserve_free_mb` 512 for small models, or retrying the load when RAM frees up.
4. **Measure 20 vs 15 again on a quiet PC.** It was not measurable here, because the audit kept the pacer at heavy.
5. **Decide whether 15 to 17 s is fine for the 15 s limit**, or check twice a second under pressure.
